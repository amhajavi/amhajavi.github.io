Research focus

I work on building systems that can carry speech directly from one voice to another, preserving not just words but timing, emotion, and nuance. My work lives in the world of speech-to-speech models, where language isn’t just text in disguise but a rich, flowing signal full of personality. I’m especially drawn to acoustic replication, trying to rebuild voices so faithfully that they feel less synthesized and more spoken into existence. Somewhere between phonetics and a bit of mischief, I like pushing models to whisper, emote, and occasionally surprise us with how human they can sound. In short, I give machines a voice—and then see how convincingly they can tell their own story.
