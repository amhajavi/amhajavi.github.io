Research agenda

Research interests

My work explores speech and audio systems that combine strong representations with practical engineering for real-world evaluation.

Speech representation learning

I study architectures that learn speaker and speech embeddings under limited-duration and noisy conditions.

Robust recognition systems

I develop models that preserve accuracy for short segments, mismatched environments, and realistic deployment scenarios.

Applied learning practice

Reproducible data pipelines, transparent evaluation, and research engineering are central to my approach.

Core themes

  • Deep learning for audio
  • Speaker verification
  • Attention and representation
  • Speech disfluency analysis
  • Signal processing for ML

Technical approach

  1. Define robust tasks. Start with realistic constraints and evaluation metrics.
  2. Build reproducible pipelines. Keep data, training, and analysis transparent.
  3. Translate to systems. Move from prototypes to stable research code.
